# Brand logo attribution

Every brand logo published in this repository is listed here with the licence that permits it,
and the Wikimedia Commons file page it came from. This is the record that makes the logo packs
reusable: a mark under CC BY-SA or CC BY carries an attribution duty, and this table is where the
author and licence for it are stated.

The logo packs in this repository are **not** covered by the repository's ODbL licence, which
applies to the store and brand databases. Each logo is under its own licence, listed below.

Most were resolved from the chain's Wikidata entity via property **P154 (logo image)**, so no
filename was guessed at, and the licence was then read from Commons' own file metadata. The few
found by searching Commons directly are listed separately at the end, with the reason.

## Copyright and trademark are separate

Most of these marks are **public domain for copyright purposes** - a wordmark generally sits below
the threshold of originality, so no copyright subsists in it and redistribution is free.
They remain **registered trademarks of their owners**. Stapel shows a logo only to identify
the card the user already holds, which is nominative use. It claims no affiliation with,
or endorsement by, any chain listed here.

A chain that would rather not see its logo here only has to ask.
